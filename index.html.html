What we know for sure about U & ME Burger Hub.

Where

Bardibas-1, Mahottari, Madhesh Province, Nepal – on the BP Highway town of Bardibas.

Burgers and fries, chicken and veg biryani, fried rice, Newari khaja sets and a long list of veg starters.

Questions

Only answers we can stand behind.

Where is U & ME Burger Hub?

Bardibas-1, Mahottari, Nepal. Use the Google Maps link in the Visit section.

What can I eat?

See the menu above: veg starters, biryani, fried rice and Newari khaja sets, plus burgers and fries.

Is there vegetarian food?

Yes. Veg starters, veg biryani, veg fried rice and a veg khaja set are on the menu.

Can I order online?

You can build an order on this page, but sending it online isn’t connected yet. In your cart, tap “Copy order”, then “Open chat” and paste it into Messenger. Or show the cart at the counter.

Opening hours, phone and payment?

Phone: 9827891939. Open every day, 6:00 AM – 8:00 PM. On festivals and special occasions hours may change, so please call before visiting. Payment methods haven’t been published yet. Please check our Facebook page or ask at the restaurant.
